# Reconditioning Standard Operating Procedure

The reconditioning team must record a start time when a vehicle enters the workshop. The assigned technician must keep the job status current and document any blocker in the job record. A blocker should describe the specific dependency, such as a part awaiting delivery, an inspection result, supplier work, or manager approval.

Vehicles that remain in reconditioning for five calendar days or longer must be reviewed by the branch manager. The review should confirm the current job status, the documented blocker, the responsible employee, and the next action. The assistant must not infer a blocker when the job record is empty; it should report that no blocker was recorded.

Before a vehicle is marked ready for sale, the assigned technician must complete the applicable quality checks and record the result. If additional repair work is discovered, the technician must document the finding and request approval when the work exceeds the branch's delegated authority.
