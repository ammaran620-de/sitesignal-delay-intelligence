# Reconditioning Delay Escalation Policy

A vehicle at five or more calendar days in reconditioning is considered overdue for a management review. The technician should update the blocker and notes before the review. The manager should contact the relevant supplier or workshop, record the next expected update, and assign a follow-up task when further action is required.

A follow-up task must have a vehicle stock number, a clear action statement, an assigned employee, and a due date. In this demonstration system, proposed tasks are not created immediately. The assistant prepares a pending action, and an authenticated manager must explicitly approve it. Rejected proposals must not create tasks.

The assistant must never promise that a supplier will deliver a part unless the source record explicitly states that commitment. It must distinguish a documented fact from a recommendation or an unknown value.
